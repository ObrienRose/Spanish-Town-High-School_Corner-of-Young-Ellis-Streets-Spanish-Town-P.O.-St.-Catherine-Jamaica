About Spanish Town High School

STHS describes itself as “The Oasis of Intelligence.” The school promotes a lifelong love of learning and encourages students to aim for excellence.

Mission

The school aims to provide a safe, motivating learning environment where students and staff can reach their potential and develop pride, self-worth, responsibility, and commitment to their families, country and wider community.

Vision

STHS seeks to provide high-quality education that builds worthwhile values and prepares students for society's changing needs and education transformation goals.

School Location

Corner of Young and Ellis Streets, Spanish Town, St. Catherine, Jamaica.

School History and Milestones

The official school history dates STHS to January 1967, when it opened as a Junior Secondary School under Principal Lebert Dennis. The school website describes current enrolment as about 1,300 students, admitted mainly through the Primary Exit Profile (PEP).

  1. 1974 - Secondary School

    The school was upgraded to a Secondary School and introduced the shift system.

  2. 1995 - Comprehensive High School

    STHS became a Comprehensive High School. The school colours and crest were introduced that year.

  3. 2000 - High School Status

    The school was officially upgraded to High School status.

School Motto and Crest

Aim for Excellence

The school history identifies “Aim for Excellence” as the school motto. It says the crest was introduced in 1995 by past student Errol McGlashing and represents industrial technology, vocational skills, sports and academic achievement.

The official school colours are maroon, gold and grey.

Sixth Form

The Sixth Form offers a CAPE academic pathway and technical and vocational programmes, including Hospitality and Tourism Management and Nail Enhancement.

CAPE subjects listed by the school include Agricultural Science, Biology, Caribbean Studies, Chemistry, Communication Studies, Entrepreneurship, Environmental Science, Financial Accounts, Food and Nutrition, Information Technology, Management of Business, Physics, Sociology and Tourism. The technical and vocational options also include City & Guilds and Employability Skills.
